HOW TO REPLACE YOUR CV LATER

1. Open this folder:
   assets/cv/

2. Delete or overwrite:
   Vahram_Tumanyan_CV.pdf

3. Put your new PDF in this same folder.

4. IMPORTANT:
   Keep the exact filename:
   Vahram_Tumanyan_CV.pdf

You do NOT need to edit index.html, CSS, or JavaScript.
The DOWNLOAD CV button will automatically download the new PDF.
