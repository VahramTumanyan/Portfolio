FLY AUTO-HIDE V5

Only the fly behavior was changed.

Behavior:
- In the upper site / portfolio / work area, the fly works normally.
- When ABOUT reaches the visible part of the screen, the fly automatically disappears.
- It stays hidden through ABOUT and CONTACT.
- If the visitor scrolls back above ABOUT, the fly returns automatically IF it was enabled.
- The fly's manual toggle still works.
- This automatic rule affects ONLY the fly.
- Other/future animation demos and their controls are not disabled by this scroll rule.
