HOW TO REPLACE A COVER YOURSELF
1. Open assets/private-covers/
2. Replace the correct file with your new image.
3. Keep the SAME filename.

For example:
Crazy Frog card cover:
assets/private-covers/crazy-frog.jpg

Paris Hilton card cover:
assets/private-covers/paris-hilton.jpg
