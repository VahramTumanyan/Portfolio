PRIVATE VIEWER ZOOM V1

Only the closed/private portfolio viewer was changed.

Controls:
- Mouse wheel over the artwork: zoom in / out.
- Double-click artwork: quick 1.65x zoom; double-click again resets.
- Mouse drag while zoomed: pan.
- Touchscreen pinch: zoom in / out.
- One-finger drag while zoomed: pan.
- Zoom range: 1.0x to 2.6x.
- Changing page/project resets zoom to 1.0x.

No other site section or design was changed.
