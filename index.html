Over the last several years in iGaming, I’ve become a highly versatile artist, working across game art, concept art, characters, environments, illustration, UI and visual development. This experience has taught me to adapt quickly to different visual styles and handle a wide range of art tasks — from early ideas and concepts through final production art.
